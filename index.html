A habit to keep

Return to the words.

You can begin before you feel ready. Keep the Chinese text in view and give a short passage more than one reading.

  1. LookNotice the length, repeated characters, and pauses.
  2. ReadUse a translation to find your bearings, then return to the Chinese.
  3. AskWrite one specific question about an image, a word, or a claim.
  4. ReturnRevisit the passage later and see what you notice next.
